Important: Raw audio recordings are first processed on your device using on-device ML models (Whisper for speech-to-text, custom TensorFlow Lite model for emotion detection). The transcribed text, emotion scores, and the original audio file are then uploaded to our secure cloud servers (Firestore and Firebase Storage) so you can access your emotion history across sessions. No raw audio is transmitted to any third party beyond our Firebase infrastructure.

We do not sell your personal information to third parties. We do not share your data with third parties for their own marketing purposes.

On-Device Processing: The following processes happen entirely on your device and no data is sent to external servers:

  • Speech-to-text transcription — via on-device Whisper model (whisper_kit)
  • Audio emotion classification — via custom TensorFlow Lite model (6 emotions)
  • Text emotion analysis — via keyword-based matching (no AI/ML API calls)

After on-device processing, the resulting transcript, emotion scores, and audio file are uploaded to Firebase for persistent storage and cross-session access.

7.1 Account Deletion

You can delete your account and all associated data directly from within the app. Follow these steps:

1

Go to the Profile page

Tap the profile icon at the bottom-right of the Home tab.

2

Tap the Delete Account button

Scroll down and tap the red "Delete Account" button.

3

Confirm deletion

Read the warning dialog, then tap "Delete" to confirm. You may be asked to re-login for verification.

4

Account deleted

After confirmation, your account and all data will be permanently deleted. You will be redirected to the login screen.

⚠️ This action is irreversible. Make sure you have saved any important data before deleting your account.

7.2 Data Deleted

The following data will be permanently deleted when your account is removed:

  • Profile Data — Name, email, and other account information.
  • Recording History — All voice recordings, transcripts, and emotion analysis results.
  • Big Five & Psychology Results — Big Five personality scores and psychological health scores.
  • Subscription Data — Premium status, purchase history, and other subscription information.
  • App Preferences — Theme, notification settings, and locale preferences.

7.3 Data NOT Deleted

The following data cannot be deleted as it is stored anonymously for analytics and service improvement:

  • Anonymous Logs — Aggregated usage statistics that cannot be linked to any individual.
  • Payment Data — Transaction information stored by third-party payment providers (RevenueCat, Google Play) is governed by their respective privacy policies.

7.4 Deletion Timeline

  • Immediate (Real-time): Profile data, recording history, and app preferences are deleted from the active database within approximately 1 minute after confirmation.
  • Maximum 7 days: All data is removed from all systems, including backups and caches, within 7 business days at the latest.

7.5 Cancelling Deletion

If you change your mind within 7 days of requesting deletion, you can log back in to cancel the request. After 7 days, data is permanently deleted and cannot be restored.
